Add two XState review rules and close detector gaps in the existing three. New: `spawned-actor-release` (one finding per machine definition that uses `spawnChild(...)` or `spawn(...)` inside `assign`; the judge pairs every spawn with a reachable `stopChild` and the removal of its ref from context) and `persisted-snapshot-compat` (`getPersistedSnapshot()` calls and `createActor`/`useActor`/`useMachine`/`useActorRef` calls restoring a `snapshot`; the judge checks versioned storage, fallback to a fresh actor, JSON-serialisable context and named actor `src`). Both join `xstatePreset`, not `starterPreset`, and skip `**/*.{test,spec}.*`.

`derived-boolean-context` now also matches `enqueue.assign(...)` and setup-bound `<x>.assign(...)`, the initial `context` and `types.context` of a machine definition, and `status|state|step|phase|mode` string-enum fields that mirror the finite state (new `stateMirrorPattern` option); its checklist adds reading `snapshot.hasTag`/`matches` and the purity of guards reached by `snapshot.can`. `actor-cleanup` excludes test files by default and its checklist covers exit actions not running on an external `stop()`, React ownership through `useActorRef`/`useMachine`/`createActorContext`, and an unsubscribe check relaxed once `stop()` is proven. `machine-failure-coverage` reports uncovered spawned actors even when an `invoke` exists (new `spawnCallPattern` and `spawnErrorMarkerPattern` options), reports empty `onError` handlers, and states that errors thrown in actions are not caught by `onError`. Standard revisions of the three rules and detector versions of `derived-boolean-context` and `machine-failure-coverage` are bumped, so earlier acceptances need a fresh review.
